The story

The first time a course I built changed nothing

It was accurate, well designed and delivered on time. People went back to the same process, the same manager and the same tools.

The gap was never only knowledge. It was how people felt about the change, and whether anything around them made it easier. Nineteen years later, that is still where I start.

Emotional intelligence

People decide with feelings first

A low completion rate, a churned customer or a disengaged team usually has an emotion behind it before it has a number. Plutchik's wheel names eight primary emotions and how they combine.

I use it to read sentiment in learner feedback, employee surveys and customer conversations, find where trust drops or fear rises, and design the fix into the product, the programme or the coaching.
